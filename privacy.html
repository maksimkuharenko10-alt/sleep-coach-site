Sleep Coach Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

In short
  • Your sleep data is stored only on your phone. We don’t have servers that receive it.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Health data from Apple Health or Health Connect is used only to show and improve your sleep plan, never for advertising.
  • You can turn off anonymous usage analytics, export your data, and delete it all in the app.

This policy explains what data the Sleep Coach app (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. Sleep Coach is provided by Maks Kukharenko (“we”, “us”). Questions: maksim.kuharenko10@gmail.com.

Sleep Coach gives general sleep-schedule guidance. It is not a medical device and doesn’t diagnose or treat any condition.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only on your device:

  • Quiz answers: age, wake and sleep times, how long you take to fall asleep, how you feel in the morning, and optional answers about your goal, caffeine, screens, naps, snoring and long-term sleep trouble.
  • Your plan and settings: sleep goal, bedtimes, reminders, wake-up alarm and sound settings.
  • Your sleep log: when you went to bed and woke up, morning ratings, and tags such as caffeine or stress.

Reminders and the wake-up alarm are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved. If you export your data or make a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

2. Apple Health and Health Connect

If you turn it on, the app reads sleep (including sleep stages), heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen and workouts from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) to fill in your sleep log and show how you slept. If you turn on “Write sleep sessions”, nights you log in the app are saved to Apple Health or Health Connect.

  • Health data is used only to provide the app’s sleep features.
  • It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with data brokers.
  • Vitals (heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen) are read and shown on your phone and are not uploaded anywhere.
  • You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app (or Health Connect) and in the app’s Settings.

3. Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

4. Usage analytics

To learn which features are used, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog (hosted in the EU), for example “opened Trends” or “started sleep sounds”. These events:

  • are not linked to your name, email or account, and no user profile is created;
  • never include sleep or wake times, durations, ratings, quiz answers, tags or health data;
  • don’t use your IP address to determine your location, and include no session recordings.

You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy → “Share anonymous usage data”.

6. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone stays until you delete it (Settings → Reset all data) or delete the app.
  • Anonymous analytics events are kept for up to 12 months.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Export: Settings → Backup & export lets you export your sleep log or a full backup at any time.
  • Delete: Settings → Reset all data deletes everything the app stores. Deleting the app does too.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Health access or analytics in Settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal data, and to object to processing. Contact us at maksim.kuharenko10@gmail.com to exercise them. If you’re in the EU or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Health data is processed only on your device, with your explicit consent when you turn on Health access. We process other data to provide the app you asked for, or for our legitimate interest in improving it (anonymous analytics, which you can turn off).

8. Children

Sleep Coach is not intended for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

9. Security

Your sleep data stays on your device, protected by your phone’s own security. The little data that is sent (subscription status and anonymous analytics) is encrypted in transit.

10. Changes

We’ll update this page if our practices change, for example if we add optional accounts to sync your data, and update the effective date above. For significant changes, we’ll let you know in the app.
